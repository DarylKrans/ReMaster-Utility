ReMaster began as a simple utility for analyzing patterns in copy-protected Commodore 64
disk images—just a way to learn how various protections worked in the '80s and '90s.

Over time, it grew into a powerful preservation tool capable of generating G64 images
from many types of protected disks that are notoriously difficult to duplicate, even with
advanced tools like Nibtools and the XUM (ZoomFloppy) adapter.

Some protections, like V-Max, use extremely short sync marks that are often misinterpreted
as data. Others, like Vorpal, employ tiny headers that can cause bit-shifting, making it
hard to determine track boundaries.

ReMaster tackles these challenges head-on. Simply import a NIB file, and it will
automatically analyze, correct, align and output a working G64 image ready for emulators
or even writing back to real disks (assuming a clean image source).

Support for checksum repair and error detection is built in for some encoding schemes with
partial or full correction available depending on the protection. Supports error checking
and correction on Vorpal, MicroProse custom format disks, RapidLok is error checing only.
